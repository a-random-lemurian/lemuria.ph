Wouldn't it be wonderful to own your own datacenter? Here's the Creed of the Personal Datacenter, something I tell myself sometimes as I continue to reach for my (probably unrealistic) dream of being a hyperscaler. Even if I can't be a hyperscaler, a large server rack will do the job just fine.


- A thousand Kubernetes pods to do as I say.
- A thousand-petabyte Ceph to remember what I know.
- A thousand CPUs to compute what I ask.
- A thousand terabytes of RAM to remember what my programs are doing.
- A thousand terabits down to download what I want.
- A thousand terabits up to show the world what I make.
